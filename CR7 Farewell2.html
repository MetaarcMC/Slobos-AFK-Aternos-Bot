The long way round

Club by club

  1. 2002–03

    Sporting CP

    A teenage winger from Madeira breaks into the Lisbon first team. Alex Ferguson sees him play and is convinced almost at once.

    31 games · 5 goals
  2. 2003–09

    Manchester United

    He inherits the number 7 shirt and turns it into a legend. Three league titles in a row, the Champions League in Moscow, and a first Ballon d'Or in 2008.

    292 games · 118 goals · 31 league goals in 2007/08
  3. 2009–18

    Real Madrid

    Nine seasons that rewrote the record books. More than a goal a game, four more European Cups, and the club's all-time leading scorer by a distance.

    438 games · 450 goals · 4 Champions Leagues
  4. 2018–21

    Juventus

    A new league, the same appetite. Two Serie A titles and a century of goals in Turin.

    134 games · 101 goals · 2 Serie A titles
  5. 2021–22

    Manchester United, again

    The return to Old Trafford, where it all began.

    54 games · 27 goals
  6. 2023–

    Al Nassr

    A new chapter in Riyadh, and still scoring. The story is not finished yet.

    Chapter in progress
